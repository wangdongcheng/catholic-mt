The story behind the domain

Why catholic.mt?

1/September/2026

I still can’t quite believe I managed to register the country-specific domain — catholic.mt.

Maybe to most people, it is just a domain name, just a bit of nerdy self-entertainment. But for me, it somehow means much more than that. I really need to think carefully about what good I can do with such an extraordinary domain name.

Malta is a place where the Christian story is inseparably connected with St Paul the Apostle. According to Acts 28, St Paul was shipwrecked on this island on his way to Rome, and Christian tradition has always remembered his arrival as the beginning of the Gospel’s presence in Malta.

And now, almost two thousand years later, a Chinese guy named after Paul somehow ended up living in Malta — and discovered that catholic.mt was still available 😂.

So I registered it.

And, naturally, the public receiving email became:

paul@catholic.mt

Yes I know. Extremely nerdy.

But sometimes a tiny thing on the internet can carry a surprisingly deep personal meaning, who knows.
